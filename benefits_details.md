*Guidelines for the proper use of the SSC BatStateU–Alangilan Membership Card and the availment of partner benefits*

**SSC Alangilan Membership Card: General Guidelines**

These guidelines establish the proper use of the SSC BatStateU–Alangilan Membership Card and provide cardholders with clear instructions for availing of the benefits offered by partner establishments.

**I. Membership Card Presentation and Verification**

**1\. Presentation of Membership Card.** Cardholders must present their valid SSC BatStateU–Alangilan Membership Card before completing a transaction to avail of applicable discounts and privileges.

**2\. Membership Verification.** Partner establishments may verify the authenticity and validity of the membership card through the designated verification system.

**II. Proper Use of the Membership Card**

**3\. Personal Use Only.** The membership card is strictly for the personal use of the registered cardholder. Lending, sharing, transferring, or allowing unauthorized individuals to use the card is prohibited.

**4\. Security and Protection.** Cardholders are responsible for keeping their digital membership card and its associated QR code secure. Unauthorized duplication, alteration, or distribution of the membership card is prohibited.

**5\. Proper Conduct.** Cardholders must maintain respectful and professional conduct when interacting with partner establishments and their personnel. Misuse of the membership card or abuse of partner privileges may result in the denial of benefits.

**III. Availing of Partner Benefits**

**6\. Compliance with Partner Establishment Policies.** Cardholders must follow the specific terms and conditions set by each partner establishment, including minimum purchase requirements, applicable services, discount rates, and other restrictions.

**7\. Validity of Benefits.** All discounts and privileges are subject to the validity period and conditions specified in the respective Memoranda of Agreement. Cardholders are encouraged to confirm the availability of benefits before making a purchase or booking a service.

**8\. Non-Combination of Discounts.** Membership benefits may not be combined with other ongoing promotions, discounts, or special offers unless expressly permitted by the partner establishment.

**IV. Concerns and Assistance**

**9\. Reporting of Issues.** Any concerns regarding membership verification, incorrect membership information, or the denial of applicable benefits should be reported to the Supreme Student Council (SSC) BatStateU–Alangilan for proper assistance.

**10\. Clarification of Benefits.** For questions regarding specific discounts, privileges, or restrictions, cardholders should coordinate with the concerned partner establishment or the SSC BatStateU–Alangilan.
