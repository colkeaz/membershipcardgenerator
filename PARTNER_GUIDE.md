# SSC-AL Membership Portal: Partner Guide

Thank you for partnering with the **Supreme Student Council, Alangilan Campus, BatStateU**.

This guide explains how the digital membership card system works so your staff can easily verify student memberships and apply the agreed-upon benefits.

## What is the Digital Membership Card?

Instead of physical ID cards, the SSC-AL provides an official digital membership card. This card is hosted on a secure web portal and includes:
- The student's Name and SR-Code.
- Their College/Department.
- A **Verification QR Code**.

## How to Verify a Student's Membership

When a student requests a discount or perk at your establishment, they will present their digital membership card on their phone.

There are two ways to verify it:

1. **Visual Check**: Ensure the card looks authentic, displays the student's name, and matches the official layout. You may ask to see their University ID if you need to double-check their identity.
2. **QR Code Scanning**: 
   - Open your smartphone camera or any QR scanner app.
   - Scan the QR code on the student's digital card.
   - It will direct you to a secure verification link on the official SSC portal, confirming whether the student's membership is active and valid.

## Providing Discounts

Once verified, simply apply the discount or perk you have generously agreed to provide to SSC-AL members. Your partnership details and specific offers are advertised to all students within the portal's Benefits Section.
