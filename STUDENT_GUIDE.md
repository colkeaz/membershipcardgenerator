# SSC-AL Membership Portal: Student Guide

Welcome to the **Official Membership Portal of the Supreme Student Council, Alangilan Campus, BatStateU**.

As a bonafide student, you can use this portal to generate your official digital membership card and browse exclusive benefits.

## How to Get Your Digital Card

1. **Access the Portal**: Go to the official link provided by the SSC (e.g., `https://membershipcardgen.web.app/`).
2. **Enter Your Details**: You will be prompted to enter your Name, SR-Code, and Department. Ensure these match your official university records.
3. **Generate**: Once validated by the system, your digital membership card will be generated. It includes a QR code that partners can scan to verify your active membership.

## How to Use Your Card

- **Save it**: You can download or screenshot the digital card to your phone for offline access.
- **Present it**: When visiting a partner establishment, simply show your digital card on your phone.
- **Scan for Verification**: Partners may scan the QR code on your card to quickly verify your membership status in real-time.

## Viewing Your Benefits

The portal features a **Benefits Section** where you can see all active partnerships, discounts, and perks available to you. 
- Keep an eye on this page, as new partners and discounts are added regularly by the SSC!

## Need Help?
If you encounter any issues generating your card or notice incorrect information, please contact the **Supreme Student Council - Alangilan Campus** via our official Facebook page or drop by the SSC office.
